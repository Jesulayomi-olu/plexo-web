Motion lab: AI card icons

Your three card icons, animated six different ways. Each style loops here so you can compare them; on the site it would play at the right moment (for example when a card lights up in the scroll spotlight). This is a preview page only, not part of the website.

1 · What "easing" means

Easing is the speed curve of a movement: same distance, same time, different feel. "Ease in and out" describes this timing, not the kind of movement. Watch how each dot travels:

LinearConstant speed. Mechanical and robotic; rarely right for UI.
Ease-inStarts slow, ends fast. Feels like accelerating away; good for things leaving.
Ease-outStarts fast, ends slow. Feels like arriving and settling; the default for entrances.
Ease-in-outSlow start, fast middle, slow end. Smooth and calm; good for loops and moving between two places.
SpringOvershoots a little, then settles, like a physical object. Playful and tactile.

2 · Kinds of icon motion

Idle loops run continuously while visible; triggered motions play once when something happens (the card lights up, or you hover); entrances play the first time something appears.

1. Float Idle loop

Each icon bobs gently up and down, forever, slightly out of step with the others. A calm, alive feeling. Usually only on the lit card, so the panel never feels busy.

2. Sway (rock) Idle loop

A slow back-and-forth tilt of a few degrees, like an object resting on a surface. Softer and quieter than a float.

3. Pop Triggered

A quick springy squash-and-stretch: it dips, overshoots slightly bigger, then settles. Plays the moment its card lights up.

4. Turn (flip) Triggered

A full turn around its vertical axis, showing off that it is a 3D object. Playful and eye-catching; once per activation.

5. Draw-on Entrance

The outlines draw themselves line by line, then the pale green fill fades in. Feels crafted and editorial. Plays the first time the card lights up.

6. Rise & settle Entrance

Starts slightly low and transparent, then rises into place with a tiny overshoot. A clean, classic entrance; plays once when the section appears.
